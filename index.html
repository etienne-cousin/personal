About

I’m a London-based life sciences strategist and operator interested in how scientific innovation becomes investable, scalable, and clinically useful.

I studied Biology at Oxford and Biotechnology at Imperial before spending four years in life sciences strategy consulting, where I got to work on some really exciting projects across the whole Life Sciences indsutry.

I now work as Head of Strategy & Operations at PreComb Therapeutics, a Series A precision oncology startup using patient-derived tumour microtissues to support drug discovery and translational research. I joined as an early commercial hire and have since worked across fundraising, board materials, investor reporting, partnerships, commercial execution, and strategic transactions.

My interests sit at the intersection of science, technology and business. I’m particularly interested in AI-enabled drug discovery, the quality of the experimental systems behind it, and the clinical and industrial bottlenecks that determine whether better science actually leads to better medicines.

This site is a home for my writing, projects, and notes on those themes. Outside of work, I’m usually reading or playing racquet sports.
