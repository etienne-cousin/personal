About

I’m a London-based life sciences strategist and operator interested in how scientific progress becomes investable, scalable, and clinically useful.

I studied Biology at Oxford and Biotechnology at Imperial before spending four years in life sciences strategy consulting, mostly at L.E.K. Consulting. There, I worked across biopharma, medtech, and healthcare on growth strategy, commercial due diligence, and market access.

I now work as Head of Strategy & Operations at PreComb Therapeutics, a Series A precision oncology startup using patient-derived tumour microtissues to support drug discovery and translational research. I joined as an early commercial hire and have since worked across fundraising, board materials, investor reporting, partnerships, commercial execution, and strategic transactions.

My interests sit at the intersection of science, data, capital allocation, and execution. I’m particularly interested in AI-enabled drug discovery, the quality of the experimental systems behind it, and the clinical and industrial bottlenecks that determine whether better science actually leads to better medicines.

This site is a home for my writing, projects, and notes on those themes. Outside of work, I’m usually reading, building small software projects, or playing racquet sports.

Background

I started my career in life sciences strategy consulting, where I learned how to take ambiguous scientific and commercial questions and turn them into structured recommendations. At L.E.K. Consulting and CB Partners, I worked across biopharma, medtech, healthcare services, pricing, market access, growth strategy, and commercial due diligence.

After consulting, I wanted to get closer to the messy reality of building companies. I spent a year as an independent consultant, working with founders, startups, corporates, and investors on strategy, fundraising, commercial diligence, and go-to-market. One project involved writing the investment memo and supporting diligence for a Tier-1 European VC on a pre-seed TechBio company, which ultimately led to an investment.

I then joined PreComb Therapeutics, a Series A precision oncology startup, as an early commercial hire. PreComb uses patient-derived tumour microtissues to help biopharma companies test drugs and combinations in more predictive biological systems. My role has been broad by design: fundraising, investor materials, board reporting, commercial partnerships, revenue generation, strategic transactions, and operational problem-solving across whatever needs moving forward.

Alongside my day job, I’ve been building TrialScope, a clinical trial intelligence platform that grew out of a problem I kept facing in consulting: understanding clinical activity around a disease area quickly, without relying on expensive enterprise tools.

Across all of this, the common thread is the same: I’m interested in the places where science, data, capital, and execution meet — and in how good ideas become investable, scalable, and clinically useful.
